# Hi, I'm Nikola!

I'm a fourth-year **Software Engineering and Information Technologies** student at the **Faculty of Technical Sciences, University of Novi Sad**.

Outside university, I've spent a lot of time creating games in Unity. What I enjoy most about programming is coming up with my own ideas and turning them into something visual and interactive, whether it's a game mechanic, an AI-controlled opponent, or a procedurally generated world. I'm also interested in the more technical, lower-level side of programming, and **C++** is one of the languages I find most interesting.

## What you'll find here

Most of my repositories fall into two groups:

- **Personal games and experiments.** I've been making games in Unity since high school. Some started as small experiments and grew far beyond what I originally planned, like [Color Attack](https://github.com/nikolasa004/ColorAttack) and [The Engineer's Arena](https://github.com/nikolasa004/TheEngineersArena).
- **University projects.** These cover a variety of topics, from algorithms and data structures to larger team-built systems, including a [Go-based LSM-tree key-value engine](https://github.com/nikolasa004/NASP-projekat) and a [plugin-based graph exploration platform](https://github.com/nikolasa004/SOK-Project).

I particularly enjoy projects where I can work out and implement the core ideas myself, rather than just put existing components together. I like having room to make technical decisions, try different approaches, and figure out why something does or doesn't work.
